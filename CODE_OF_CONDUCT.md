# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Correct mistakes without humiliating beginners.
- Separate evidence about work from judgments about a person.
- Accept constructive feedback and protect private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through GitHub. This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
